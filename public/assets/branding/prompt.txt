Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: ONE finished original desktop app icon for Openlude.
Art direction: a beautifully designed small silver fox, charming and highly recognizable, treated as a premium native desktop app mascot. Prioritize beauty, warmth, and a strong distinctive silhouette. This is a fresh concept, not a letter logo or a symbol diagram.

Square canvas, preferably 1024 x 1024. One perfectly smooth deep midnight-navy rounded-square app tile with even transparent padding, occupying 84 percent of the canvas. True transparent alpha outside the tile.
The fox: a compact elegant silver-white fox in a gentle three-quarter profile, curled into a comfortable seated pose. Its large fluffy tail sweeps up around one side of its body in a single graceful crescent, with a tiny subtle warm apricot tip. Two pointed ears, one small dark expressive eye, a little charcoal nose, and a short clean muzzle. A poised, quietly curious expression, looking slightly upward. The face and ears are clearly separated from the tail so the silhouette stays readable at 32px. Avoid excessive fur strands; define fur with a few broad beautifully curved shapes. The fox fills roughly 64 percent of the canvas and feels balanced, not cramped.
Style: polished stylized illustration with very gentle sculptural volume, somewhere between a carefully crafted enamel object and a modern editorial mascot. Smooth clean contours, soft porcelain-white and cool silver planes, restrained warm peach in the inner ear and tail tip. Not photorealistic, not plastic toy, not chibi, not anime. Tiny purposeful facial features, sophisticated adult taste.
Palette: midnight navy #142438 for the tile, silver-white #EEF1EF and cool mist gray for the fox, very small warm apricot accents #E7AE84. No saturated neon colors. Neutral soft lighting from upper left, subtle depth inside the tile, no outer cast shadow.
The tile stays calm and mostly flat. Do not add stars, particles, scenery, a moon, a ring, an orbit, or a halo. The fox itself is the entire identity.
No text or letters. No O monogram, no detached geometric segments, no arrows, no play button, no power symbol, no curtains, no folded paper, no stacked cards, no clocks, no rainbow gradient, no chrome, no glowing edges, no watermark, no presentation board or multiple variants.
Ensure the fox is an original silver-fox character, not the Firefox logo: no orange fox, no globe, no encircling a ball, no flame-like tail.
Deliver one exceptionally clean, aesthetically refined app icon with a crisp alpha boundary and no stray pixels outside its rounded-square silhouette.
